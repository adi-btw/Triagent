## Summary
On 2025-11-18 at 00:00 UTC, 100% of payment authorizations failed for 38
minutes. checkout-api showed "Payment could not be processed" for every order.
Classified SEV1: payments fully unavailable.

## Root cause
The client certificate payments-api uses for mTLS to the card vault expired at
its notAfter time. cert-manager had been failing to renew it for 30 days
because the `ClusterIssuer` credentials were rotated without updating the
secret; the Certificate showed `READY=False` but nothing alerted on it. Logs
showed `x509: certificate has expired or is not yet valid` on every vault call.

## Resolution and follow-ups
Fixed the issuer secret, ran `cmctl renew`, and restarted payments-api pods to
load the new certificate. Action item AI-455: alert on certificates expiring
in under 14 days. Action item AI-456: alert on any Certificate with
`READY=False` for more than 1 hour.
