## Summary
On 2026-04-02, checkout-api error rate reached 64% for 12 minutes during a
deploy of v2.19.0. Classified SEV1: more than 50% errors on a revenue path.

## Root cause
The release ran a migration `CREATE INDEX idx_orders_customer ON orders
(customer_id)` without `CONCURRENTLY`, taking a lock that blocked all writes
to `orders`. checkout-api requests waited on the lock, held their pool
connections, and new requests failed with `pool.acquire timed out after
5000ms`. orders-db had only 88 of 200 connections in use, so the first
responder's suspicion of connection exhaustion (INC-2025-014) was wrong;
`pg_stat_activity` showed 80 sessions with `wait_event_type = Lock`.

## Resolution and follow-ups
Terminated the migration backend with `pg_terminate_backend`; errors cleared in
under a minute. Rebuilt the index with `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY` off-peak.
Action item AI-560: CI lint that rejects non-concurrent index creation on
tables over 1M rows. Action item AI-561: default `lock_timeout = '3s'` for
migrations.
