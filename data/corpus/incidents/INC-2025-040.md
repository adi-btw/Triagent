## Summary
On 2025-12-09, inventory-api showed stale stock levels for about 70 minutes
while orders-db replica lag peaked at 910 seconds. No requests failed; around
1.5% of product pages showed items as in stock after they had sold out.
Classified SEV3: inventory-api is not a revenue path in POL-SEV-001, and
checkout re-validates stock against the primary at purchase time.

## Root cause
A one-off backfill updated 60M rows of `order_items` in a single transaction on
the primary, generating about 40 GB of WAL. The replica's volume hit its IOPS
ceiling and replay fell behind; `pg_stat_replication` showed `replay_lsn`
far behind `sent_lsn` while `state` stayed `streaming`.

## Resolution and follow-ups
Cancelled the backfill and set `READ_FROM_REPLICA=false` on inventory-api until
lag returned under 30s. Re-ran the backfill in 5,000-row batches overnight.
Action item AI-470: automatic read fallback to the primary when lag exceeds 30s.
