## Summary
On 2026-03-12 from 09:00 UTC, 27% of checkout-api requests and 31% of cart-api
requests returned 401 for 19 minutes; users were bounced to the login page.
Classified SEV2: revenue paths degraded within the 5-50% error band.

## Root cause
The scheduled key rotation job activated the new signing key `k-2026-03` at the
same moment it published it to JWKS. checkout-api and cart-api cache JWKS for
one hour, so they rejected tokens signed with the new kid:
`invalid token: kid "k-2026-03" not found in JWKS`. The error rate tracked the
share of users who had refreshed their token since 09:00.

## Resolution and follow-ups
Re-activated the previous key with `authctl keys activate k-2025-12` and
restarted checkout-api and cart-api to refresh JWKS. Action item AI-540:
rotation job must publish, wait 2x the JWKS cache TTL, then activate.
