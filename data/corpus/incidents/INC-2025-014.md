## Summary
On 2025-08-21, checkout-api error rate reached 22% for 31 minutes after a
traffic spike triggered HPA scale-out from 8 to 16 pods.

## Root cause
Each checkout-api pod opens a pool of 15 connections (`DB_POOL_SIZE=15`).
At 16 pods, demand was 240 connections against max_connections=200 on orders-db.
New pods could not connect and returned 500s.

## Resolution and follow-ups
Capped HPA maxReplicas at 12 as a stopgap. Action item AI-311: introduce
PgBouncer before raising the replica cap. Action item AI-312: alert when
pg_stat_activity exceeds 85% of max_connections.
