## Summary
On 2026-01-27, 11% of payments-api authorization calls failed for 52 minutes.
p95 provider latency rose from 420ms to the 5000ms client timeout. Classified
SEV2: revenue path with errors between 5% and 50%.

## Root cause
PayFlux had a partial outage in one of its regions, returning 504 and hanging
connections. Logs showed `httpx.ReadTimeout: provider call exceeded 5000ms`.
The on-call engineer initially followed the DNS runbook because the 5000ms
timeout matched; CoreDNS was healthy and only calls to `api.payflux.example`
were slow, which ruled DNS out after 15 minutes.

## Resolution and follow-ups
Failed over with `paymentsctl provider set-primary ledgerly`; errors fell to
0.8% (Amex cards, which Ledgerly does not support). Failed back to PayFlux 3
hours later. Action item AI-512: open the circuit breaker automatically when
provider p95 exceeds 3000ms for 2 minutes. Action item AI-513: add a
per-provider latency panel to the on-call dashboard.
