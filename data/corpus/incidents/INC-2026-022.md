## Summary
On 2026-08-04 the `PgDataVolumeUsage > 80%` alert fired on orders-db at 81%;
usage had grown from 55% over three days. No queries failed and no customer
impact occurred. Classified SEV4: no customer impact, handled during business
hours.

## Root cause
A reporting replica decommissioned on 2026-08-01 left its replication slot
`reporting_replica_1` behind. With the slot inactive, the primary retained all
WAL since that date (about 96 GB in `pg_wal/`).

## Resolution and follow-ups
Confirmed with the data team that the replica was gone, ran
`SELECT pg_drop_replication_slot('reporting_replica_1');` and `CHECKPOINT;`.
Usage fell to 54% within 10 minutes. Action item AI-610: set
`max_slot_wal_keep_size = 50GB`. Action item AI-611: decommission checklist
must include dropping the slot.
