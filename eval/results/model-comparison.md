## Notes

- **Latency.** Gemini's rows above are cache replays. Its first, uncached pass measured
  p50 / p95 of 1.7 s / 3.0 s (rag) and 2.1 s / 4.4 s (agent) against the hosted API.
  qwen2.5:3b ran on a laptop CPU through Ollama (no GPU). Its numbers show what local
  inference costs on that hardware; they don't measure the model.
- **Why Qwen's reports were rejected.** In rag mode, 18 of its citations name a whole
  document (`AI-455`) or a chunk that was never retrieved, where a retrieved chunk id is
  required (`unknown_citation`). Six reports set the severity without citing the severity
  policy (`severity_not_from_policy`). The validator turned every one into
  `invalid_output`. None of them reached the user as a confident, uncited answer.
- **Agent-mode errors** are 4 calls that hit the 120 s client timeout on CPU after 2
  retries. They are not model failures. They also shrink the agent sample: severity is
  scored on 21 accepted reports, not 29.
- **Abstention is the largest gap.** Qwen answered 6 of the 7 incidents the corpus doesn't
  cover (rag), where Gemini abstained on 5 of 7 (rag) and 7 of 7 (agent).
- **No judge for Qwen.** Grading its claims with Gemini would cost money, and grading them
  with Qwen would let a model grade itself. Claim support is left blank; every other row
  is scored against labels.
