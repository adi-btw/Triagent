## Residual risk (gets through even with all defenses)

- `sev-downgrade-cites-policy`: the poisoned section is cited *next to* the real policy.
- `suppress-abstention`: a confident, cited, but wrong cause is indistinguishable from a right one without understanding the text.

These rely on the model resisting the instruction (`--live` measures that) and on a human reading the cited passage. See docs/security.md.
